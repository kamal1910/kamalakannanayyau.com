## Why AI certifications matter now

In 2026 the major vendors rebuilt their AI certifications around generative AI and agents, retired older ML exams, and a separate governance category grew fast. Picking the right one now depends more on your role and platform than ever.

Demand for AI skills is real. PwC's analysis of nearly a billion job ads found workers with AI skills earn a 56% wage premium, double the 25% premium a year earlier. Postings requiring AI skills grew 7.5% while total postings fell 11.3% ([Fast.io summary of PwC](https://www.fast.io/resources/best-ai-certifications-2026.md)).

Freelance markets show the same pattern. Upwork reports demand for AI-enabled skills rose 109% year over year, with AI integration up 178% and chatbot development up 71% ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/en-gb/resources/ai-certifications)).

## Cloud vendor certifications

Cloud certs remain the most widely recognized by employers, and all three providers overhauled their AI tracks this year.

### AWS

The Machine Learning Specialty is retired. Its replacement path is AI Practitioner (fundamentals, about $100), Machine Learning Engineer Associate (deploying and operating ML), and Generative AI Developer Professional (RAG, vector databases, Bedrock) ([Training Camp](https://trainingcamp.com/articles/whats-changing-with-aws-certifications-in-2026-and-what-to-take-instead/)).

- **Generative AI Developer Professional:** $300, 204 minutes, 85 questions ([Digicomp](https://digicomp.ch/certification/it-provider-certifications/amazon-web-services-certificates/aws-certified-generative-ai-developer-professional)).
- **Timing note:** the ML Engineer Associate is moving to version MLA-C02, in English beta now, with general availability on January 14, 2027 ([AWS blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/training-and-certification/september-2026-new-offerings/)).

### Microsoft Azure

Microsoft retired AI-102 on June 30, 2026 and replaced it with AI-103, the Azure AI App and Agent Developer Associate. DP-100 gave way to AI-300 (MLOps Engineer), and AI-900 to AI-901 ([Vlad Talks Tech](https://vladtalkstech.com/microsoft-learning-and-credential-news/microsoft-certification-retirements-2026/)).

AI-103 is not a renamed AI-102. It is built on Microsoft Foundry and puts generative AI and agents at its center ([Leland](https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/azure-ai-engineer-certification)). Skip old AI-102 study material.

### Google Cloud

- **Professional Machine Learning Engineer:** one of the most respected ML credentials. The 2026 version adds generative AI topics such as Model Garden, Vertex AI Agent Builder and RAG ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/andy_youtube_371fe0c1a37e/mastering-the-google-professional-machine-learning-engineer-exam-in-2026-om1)).
- **Generative AI Leader:** a business credential for decision-makers. It does not validate coding or production AI skills ([Trainocate](https://marketing.trainocate.com/?p=12601)).

## AI platform and model vendor certifications

These certs prove depth on one AI platform. They pay off most when your employer or clients already use that platform.

### Anthropic (Claude)

Anthropic offers four proctored exams: Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations and Architect Professional. Each runs 120 minutes, needs a scaled score of 720, and is valid for 12 months. The Developer exam lists at $125 ([Training Camp](https://trainingcamp.com/articles/claude-certifications-explained-all-four-anthropic-exams-costs-and-who-can-take-them/)).

The catch: exams are open only to members of the Claude Partner Network, which is free for firms to join ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications)). Individuals without a partner-firm email cannot register. Free Anthropic Academy course certificates are open to everyone.

### Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate

This exam costs $200, runs 90 minutes, has no prerequisites and stays valid for two years. It tests building RAG applications and LLM chains with Vector Search, Model Serving, MLflow and Unity Catalog ([Databricks exam guide](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2026-03/Databricks-Certified-Generative-AI-Engineer-Associate-Exam-Guide-Mar26.pdf)). The March 2026 update added agent development ([Trainocate](https://marketing.trainocate.com/?p=12384)).

### NVIDIA Associate: Generative AI and LLMs

An entry-level cert covering LLM fundamentals, diffusion models, prompt engineering and responsible deployment. It suits engineers moving into AI and people working close to models and GPU infrastructure ([CloudThat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/top-nvidia-certifications-for-ai-ml-generative-ai-professionals-in-2026)).

### OpenAI

Not a real option for developers yet. OpenAI Academy's free courses are open, but the formal exam-based credential has no announced date or price ([FindSkill](https://findskill.ai/blog/openai-certification-guide/)).

## AI governance, risk and security certifications

This is the fastest-growing non-coding category, driven by the EU AI Act, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001. IAPP research found only 1.5% of organizations feel their AI governance staffing is adequate ([InfosecTrain](https://www.infosectrain.com/?p=50099)).

- **IAPP AIGP:** the de facto standard for AI governance roles. It costs $649 for members or $799 for non-members, has no prerequisites, and renews with 20 CPE credits every two years ([Bigeye](https://www.bigeye.com/blog/best-ai-certifications-in-2026)).
- **ISACA AAIA, AAISM and AAIR:** each one builds on an existing ISACA-level credential. AAIA is for auditors holding CISA, AAISM for security managers with CISM or CISSP, and AAIR for risk professionals with CRISC ([Training Camp](https://trainingcamp.com/articles/which-ai-certifications-will-actually-matter-in-2026/)).

If you work in privacy, legal or compliance, start with AIGP. If you already hold an ISACA or ISC2 credential, add the matching ISACA AI cert.

## Recommendations by role

- **Developer building AI apps:** the GenAI developer cert for your cloud, either AWS Generative AI Developer Professional or Azure AI-103. Add Databricks if you work on a data platform.
- **ML or MLOps engineer:** Google Professional ML Engineer, AWS ML Engineer Associate, or Azure AI-300.
- **Consultant at a partner firm:** Claude or cloud-vendor partner certs, usually paid for by your employer.
- **Non-technical or manager:** AWS AI Practitioner, Azure AI-901, or Google Generative AI Leader.
- **Privacy, legal or compliance:** IAPP AIGP.
- **Security, audit or risk:** the ISACA AI cert that matches your existing credential.

## Are certifications worth the money?

A certification is worth paying for when it matches the platform your target employers actually use. A generic AI badge rarely moves a hiring decision.

- **Match the stack.** An AWS cert helps at an AWS shop and does little at an Azure one.
- **Check the version.** Many 2025 exams were retired or replaced in 2026. Confirm the current exam code before buying prep material.
- **Count renewal costs.** Some credentials expire in 12 months, others in two years.
- **Ask your employer.** Partner firms often pay for exams or get discounts.
- **Pair it with a project.** A working RAG app or agent on GitHub that you can explain in an interview usually carries more weight than the badge alone.

If you are only after the knowledge, the free courses from AWS, Microsoft Learn, Google, Anthropic Academy and OpenAI Academy cover much of the same material at no cost.
