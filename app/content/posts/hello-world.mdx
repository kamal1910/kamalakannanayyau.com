## How this site works

The site is built with **React 19** and **React Router 8** in framework mode:

- Most pages are **pre-rendered at build time**, so they load as plain HTML.
- The [/now](/now) page is **server-rendered on each request**. Its static shell arrives first and live data streams in afterwards with `<Suspense>`.
- Posts like this one are written in **MDX** and validated with `zod`.
